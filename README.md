# HR Attrition/Workforce Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing employee attrition using an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard built in **Microsoft Power BI**. The dashboard provides insights into workforce demographics, salary distribution, employee tenure, and attrition patterns to help identify employee groups that may be at a higher risk of leaving an organization.

The objective is to transform HR data into meaningful visual insights that support data-driven decision-making and help organizations better understand employee turnover.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall employee attrition and workforce distribution.
- Identify employee groups with higher attrition rates.
- Explore attrition patterns across age groups, gender, and marital status.
- Analyze attrition across different job roles and departments.
- Develop an interactive dashboard to monitor key HR metrics.
- Generate insights that can support employee retention strategies.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization.
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Creating calculated measures and KPIs
- **Interactive Slicers** – Filtering data by department and job role.
- **Data Visualization** – Presenting HR metrics through charts, KPI cards, and donut charts.

## 📌 Dashboard Features

### 1. HR Overview

The dashboard provides a snapshot of key workforce metrics, including:

- Total employee count
- Active and inactive employees
- Average salary
- Maximum and minimum salary
- Average employee tenure
- Average salary hike percentage
- Overall attrition rate
- High-risk employee count

### 2. Employee Attrition Analysis

This section explores attrition patterns across demographic groups:

- **Age Group:** Compares attrition across different employee age categories.
- **Gender:** Examines attrition patterns among male and female employees.
- **Marital Status:** Analyzes attrition among single, married, and divorced employees.

### 3. Attrition by Job Role and Department

Visualizations highlight job roles and departments with higher attrition percentages, helping identify workforce segments that may require further investigation.

### 4. Attrition Drivers & Insights

A dedicated report page presents additional insights into potential attrition drivers and employee retention risks.

### 5. Interactive Dashboard

Department and job role slicers allow users to filter the report dynamically and explore attrition patterns across different employee segments.

## 🔍 Key Insights

Based on the dashboard visualizations:

- **Age group:** Employees aged 18–25 show the highest attrition percentage among the displayed age groups.
- **Gender:** Male employees show slightly higher attrition than female employees.
- **Marital status:** Single employees account for the largest share of attrition in the displayed breakdown.
- **Job roles:** Sales Representatives have the highest attrition percentage among the displayed job roles.
- **Departments:** The Sales department has the highest attrition percentage among the displayed departments.

These findings help identify areas for further investigation. Additional analysis is needed to establish the underlying reasons for employee turnover.

## 📈 Business Value

The dashboard demonstrates how Power BI can be used to turn HR data into actionable insights. It can help HR teams:

- Identify employee segments that may benefit from targeted retention initiatives.
- Investigate departments and job roles with elevated turnover.
- Monitor key workforce metrics in a centralized report.
- Support evidence-based HR planning and decision-making.
